\section{Mathematical Formulation of the 11-Stage CHORD-SIEVES Pipeline}
\subsection{3-Zone Local Quadratic Flank Bridge (Stages 1--3)}
Given a candidate transit center $t_c$ and transit duration $D$ (in days) on a TESS light curve $(t_k, f_k)$, CHORD-SIEVES extracts the local neighborhood $\lvert t_k - t_c \rvert \le 2.2 D$ and enforces a strict \textbf{Zero-Gap Contiguity Gate}:
\begin{equation}
  \max_{k} \left(t_{k+1} - t_k\right) \le 0.014\text{ d} \quad (\approx 20.16\text{ min}).
\end{equation}
Any window straddling a spacecraft downlink gap, reaction-wheel momentum dump, or cadence dropout $> 0.014\text{ d}$ is immediately vetoed. The window is partitioned into three disjoint zones:
\begin{align}
  \mathcal{Z}_{\text{core}} &= \left\{ k \;\middle|\; \lvert t_k - t_c \rvert \le 0.40 D \right\}, \quad N_{\text{core}} = \lvert \mathcal{Z}_{\text{core}} \rvert \ge 12, \\
  \mathcal{Z}_{\text{flank}} &= \left\{ k \;\middle|\; 0.85 D \le \lvert t_k - t_c \rvert \le 2.20 D \right\}, \quad N_{\text{flank}} = \lvert \mathcal{Z}_{\text{flank}} \rvert \ge 40, \\
  \mathcal{Z}_{\text{in/eg}} &= \left\{ k \;\middle|\; 0.40 D < \lvert t_k - t_c \rvert < 0.85 D \right\}.
\end{align}
 furthermore, both left and right flanks must independently contain at least $15$ cadences ($N_{\text{flank},L} \ge 15$, $N_{\text{flank},R} \ge 15$). A local degree-2 polynomial baseline $B(t) = c_2 t^2 + c_1 t + c_0$ is fit via ordinary least squares \emph{exclusively} on $\mathcal{Z}_{\text{flank}}$ and bridged across $\mathcal{Z}_{\text{core}}$, yielding normalized residuals:
\begin{equation}
  r_k = \frac{f_k}{B(t_k)} - 1.0.
\end{equation}
Robust flank noise $\sigma_{\text{flank}}$ is estimated via the Gaussian-consistent Median Absolute Deviation:
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_{\text{flank}} = 1.4826 \cdot \operatorname{median}_{k \in \mathcal{Z}_{\text{flank}}}\! \left\lvert r_k - \operatorname{median}_{j \in \mathcal{Z}_{\text{flank}}}(r_j) \right\rvert.
\end{equation}
The detrended core transit depth and single-epoch signal-to-noise ratio are:
\begin{equation}
  d_{\text{PDC}} = -\operatorname{median}_{k \in \mathcal{Z}_{\text{core}}}(r_k) > 0, \qquad

\end{equation}
Across $M \ge 3$ independent transit epochs with individual ratios $\text{SNR}_m$, the quadrature multi-epoch signal-to-noise ratio must satisfy:
\begin{equation}
  \text{SNR}_{\text{comb}} = \sqrt{\sum_{m=1}^{M} \text{SNR}_m^2} \ge 9.5.
